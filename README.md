# Gothika

**Your personal YouTube library and music in Stremio and on the web.**

## Gothika Web Player

https://https-gothika-music.onrender.com/

## Google YouTube Sign-In

https://https-gothika-music.onrender.com/auth/youtube

Connect your Google account to access your YouTube playlists, liked videos, subscriptions, and music.

## Stremio Add-on

**Public manifest:**

https://https-gothika-music.onrender.com/manifest.json

**Personal manifest:**

After signing in with Google, Gothika generates your personal manifest URL:

`https://https-gothika-music.onrender.com/u/YOUR_ACCOUNT_ID/manifest.json`

Copy the personal manifest URL from the Google sign-in success page and install it in Stremio.

## Stremio Catalogues

- My YouTube Playlists
- My Liked Videos
- My Subscriptions
- YouTube Videos

## Features

- Personal YouTube library
- Google account connection
- YouTube playlists
- Liked videos
- Subscriptions
- Trending YouTube videos
- External YouTube playback
- Separate Gothika Web Player
